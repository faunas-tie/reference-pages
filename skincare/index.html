Tips

Sunscreen

  • Wear it every morning, especially after a retinal night.
  • Outside for a long time? Put more on every 2 hours.
  • Put more on after you sweat, swim or towel off.
  • The d'Alba spray does not replace sunscreen.

If pimples appear

  • Keep going with this routine, then check your skin around 31 December.
  • A few spots? Ask a pharmacist about Benzac AC 2.5% gel, and mention the retinal.
  • Across the face? Try The Ordinary Azelaic Acid 10%. Don't use it on retinal nights.
  • Also try a different moisturiser. The Capsule Cream is the first product to change.
  • Deep or painful pimples: see a GP.
